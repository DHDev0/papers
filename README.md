# Research Repository

Welcome to this repository containing research manuscripts, working papers, and theoretical ideas spanning across algorithmic complexity, the Riemann Hypothesis, and the Chowla conjectures. 

The repository is structured into three main directories, representing different stages of the research lifecycle:

## 1. PUBLISH (Finished Papers)
This folder contains the finalized, publication-ready manuscripts. These papers have undergone rigorous review and formalization of their mathematical frameworks.

**Contents include:**
- **The Algorithmic Entropy of the Critical Line:** A rigorous mathematical framework bridging the Riemann Hypothesis, P ≠ NP, and Algebraic Circuit Complexity. It establishes the Riemann Hypothesis as a geometric barrier enforcing computational hardness using Hardness vs. Randomness, pseudorandomness, and #P-hard multivariate étale cohomology.
- **EML NAND Duality & Circuit Complexity Extensions:** Manuscripts exploring the logical architecture and duality of EML NAND circuits and their implications for circuit complexity.

## 2. IN_PROGRESS (Papers in Development)
This directory houses the current working papers and manuscript suites that are actively being formalized, refined, and expanded.

**Contents include:**
- **The Even Chowla Manuscript Suite:** A comprehensive suite of papers working towards formalizing an innovative representation-theoretic approach to the Even Chowla Conjecture. This includes exploration of dynatomic fields, spectral bounds via the Motohashi-Kuznetsov framework, the Sarnak Bypass, and polynomial Chowla architectures.
- **Dynamical Trace Formulas & Arboreal Galois Representations:** Work mapping Möbius parity to the symmetric group's representation theory.
- **Nonstandard Analysis & The Scale Transfer Problem:** Investigations into structural maps, topological obstructions, and why certain analytical methods succeed or fail across different scales.

## 3. DB_IDEA (Current Ideas & Scratchpads)
This folder serves as the incubator for new ideas, raw notes, structural fixes, and unintegrated mathematical proofs.

**Contents include:**
- Raw research diaries, brainstorming documents (`expand.txt`), and missing proof fragments (`miss.md`, `miss0.md`, etc.) that are waiting to be rigorously formalized and integrated into the main manuscripts.
